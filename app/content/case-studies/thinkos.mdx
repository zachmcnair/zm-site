ThinkOS is a local-first operating system for the agentic web: private by default, yet able to give AI agents real access to the blockchain. I owned the design system and product design, and built the front end — turning a deeply technical, crypto-native idea into something calm enough for anyone to trust.

## The brief

ThinkOS set out to answer a hard question: how do you let AI agents act on your behalf — including on-chain — without sending your data to the cloud? The product had to make "your machine, your keys" feel effortless to people who aren't crypto-native.

## Approach

Local-first changed the whole posture, so I designed for "your machine, your keys" first. The home leads with a single calm prompt — not a dashboard of jargon — so the first thing you feel is control, not complexity.
